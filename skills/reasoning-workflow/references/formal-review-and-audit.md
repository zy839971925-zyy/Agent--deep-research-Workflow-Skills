# Formal review and audit

**Trigger:** Systematic/scoping review, claim-by-claim audit, methodology audit, controlled-change audit, or independent quality review is requested/warranted.

**Reads:** existing claims/artifacts, evidence records, methodology, requirements, state/change records, verification results.

**Updates:** audit findings, severity/blocker status, missing evidence/coverage, corrective actions/reopen decisions.

**May invalidate:** claims, approvals, delivery readiness, change closure.

**Exit:** material findings are dispositioned or explicitly left open with impact and next action.

**Related:** [evidence and provenance](evidence-and-provenance.md), [traceability and integrity](traceability-and-integrity.md), [change governance and effectiveness](change-governance-and-effectiveness.md).

**Return:** [root workflow](../SKILL.md) → Challenge / Reconcile / Completion.

Use only when the user requests a reproducible review, systematic/scoping synthesis, formal evidence audit, or rigorous review of an existing research product. Ordinary tasks should not inherit this ceremony.

## Formal evidence review

Define the review question, intended use, eligibility criteria, information sources, search dates, search strategy, deduplication, screening stages, extraction fields, and synthesis method before claiming reproducible or comprehensive coverage. Use a domain-appropriate question framework; PICO is not universal.

Record protocol amendments and reasons. Distinguish title/abstract from full-text screening. Keep inaccessible full texts separate from exclusions and retain exclusion reasons. Track study/report identity so multiple publications do not become multiple independent studies. Check corrections, retractions, protocols/registries, and risk of bias when relevant.

Do not claim exhaustive coverage of an open web universe or independent dual screening unless actually performed. A flow diagram or large citation count does not establish methodological compliance.

## Audit an existing answer or report

Start by extracting the material claims and their evidence dependencies. Preserve sound work; do not redo everything by default. Prioritize claims that are high-impact, weakly supported, surprising, current, quantitative, causal, accusatory, or central to downstream decisions.

Test:

- whether cited evidence entails the exact claim;
- entity/version/date/jurisdiction/population/unit alignment;
- provenance independence and copied-source clusters;
- whether inference is mislabeled as observation or fact;
- omitted counterevidence or plausible alternatives;
- causal overreach, hindsight contamination, or unsupported psychological inference;
- stale/superseded material;
- negative/completeness claims whose searched universe is undefined;
- calculations, transformations, code, or experiments claimed but not actually performed.

Escalate to fresh research only for gaps that can materially change the audit result. The audit deliverable should distinguish wrong, unsupported, outdated, out-of-scope, inaccessible, and still-uncertain claims.


## Audit reasoning quality

For Question / Reasoning work, audit the reasoning architecture as well as citations. Check:

- **problem understanding:** did the analysis identify the real epistemic task and what would count as answering it?
- **question drift:** did it solve a nearby proxy problem instead of the original one?
- **model quality:** are observation, premise, assumption, hypothesis, inference, judgment, recommendation, and unknown kept distinct where material?
- **decomposition quality:** do branches overlap unnecessarily, miss answer-bearing dependencies, or mix incompatible analytic levels?
- **recomposition:** would perfect branch answers actually imply the parent answer?
- **relationship quality:** are causal, correlational, dependency, mediation, constraint, sequence, membership, and alternative relations distinguished rather than collapsed into generic “connections”?
- **hypothesis competition:** were plausible alternative explanations tested using discriminating evidence?
- **source fitness:** were sources actually positioned to know the exact claims they support?
- **uncertainty quality:** is uncertainty attributed to missing evidence, conflict, measurement, scope/definition, model/premise, causality, or future behavior rather than vague hedging?
- **challenge strength:** are reversal conditions, weak premises, counterexamples, and counterevidence proportionate to the claim?
- **stopping:** did inquiry stop because marginal answer gain became low, rather than because a source/search quota was reached?

A well-cited answer can still fail if its problem model or inference structure is wrong.

## Synthesis discipline

Formal review quality is not citation volume. Preserve study/source dependence, incompatible estimands, methodological heterogeneity, and unresolved disagreement. Quantitative synthesis requires compatible inputs and justified methods; do not pool simply because numbers are available.


## Audit a controlled change or completed work

When auditing a work/change record, compare the documented baseline and approved delta to the actual current state. Check whether risk findings were dispositioned, required controls were implemented, procedures/training/ownership were updated, deviations were recorded, verification/validation evidence actually exists, and effectiveness criteria were checked at the intended time. Separate implementation fidelity from outcome effectiveness and from causal impact. Track audit anomalies to closure rather than treating the audit report itself as resolution.
